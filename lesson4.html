Lesson 4 - Sentences and Songs

Welcome to lesson 4! Hope you did a lot of practice with the last lesson because you’re going to need it here. As you saw in the title, we are going to be pronouncing entire sentences. This isn’t really any new content, mostly just an extension of your abilities, so this lesson will be fairly short with a little bit of new information. So, let’s get started!


Sentences are not very different from words; it’s just that you have to string multiple of them together. Let’s start with an example sentence just to get us into things. Don’t listen to the audio yet; you can listen to it later to check your accuracy:


Namaḥ sūryāya candrāya maṅgalāya budhāya ca


This is pretty long. But let's go word by word. For each word, separate it into its syllables. You should have been doing this a lot last lesson and the lesson before. If you did it correctly, it should look like this.

<
maṅ - ga - lā - ya bu - dhā - ya ca
